COMMANDS
A command is an instruction to the computer to carry out a specified task. In PILOT
there are about ten principal commands that instruct the computer to respond in
different ways. Each command is made up of two parts: the command label and the
action of the instruction. The command label is usually one to two letters in length,
followed by a colon (:). i
