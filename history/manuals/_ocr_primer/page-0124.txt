| You can use the SOUND command in both immediate and program mode.
Immediate Mode Sounds
Section 2 explained how to use the SOUND command in immediate mode. Like all
j other immediate mode commands as soon as you press the key, the com-
puter responds. In this case, the computer responds by generating a tone. The
sound continues to play until you either type another SOUND command, stop the
sound with a SO: 0 command, or press the key. However, you can’t make
f sounds in rapid succession unless you are a very fast typist!
