education.

Dr. Dean Brown, whose pioneering work at the Stanford Research In-

stitute Education Laboratory between 1967 and 1974 showed PILOT to a |
be a language for children’s programs, as well as for teachers.

Dr. Seymour Papert, at the LOGO Project at Massachusetts Institute of

Technology, for the development of “Turtle Geometry” and ‘Turtle |
Graphics,” which ATARI PILOT uses.
