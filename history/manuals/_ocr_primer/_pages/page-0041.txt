Figure 3-2 AUTO Mode
To leave AUTO mode, enter a blank line and press the key.
In AUTO mode you do not type line numbers. Anything entered is treated as a pro-
gram statement, and therefore executive commands may not be entered while in
AUTO mode. Of course, if the computer doesn’t understand your input it still
responds with the * ** WHAT'S THAT? * ** message. a

enter:
