7 The GO Subcommand
The GO subcommand moves the turtle forward the number of units specified in
the current THETA angle and plots a point.
i The format for the GO subcommand is:
GR: GO distance between points
4 Examples:
GR: GO 10
| GR: GO #L
The point is drawn in the current pen color.
