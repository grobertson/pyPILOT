The DRAW Subcommand * |
The DRAW subcommand draws a line the number of units specified in the direction
of THETA in the current pen color.
The format for the DRAW subcommand is:
GR: DRAW line length |
Examples:
GR: DRAW 20
GR: DRAW #X |
The line is drawn in the current PEN color.
