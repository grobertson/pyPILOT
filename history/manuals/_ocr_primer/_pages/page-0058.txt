in the ease with which it allows you to perform complex pattern-matching on user
input and then to make decisions relative to the presence or absence of a ‘match.’
With just three commands (TYPE, ACCEPT, and MATCH) you can create a
reasonably sophisticated, interactive dialogue program. This section shows you
how to write educational, instructional, and just plain fun PILOT programs using
I pattern-matching decisions.
