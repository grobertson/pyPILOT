| There are three subcommands that don’t use Cartesian or turtle geometry: CLEAR,
PEN, and QUIT. GR: CLEAR clears the graphics screen, and GR: QUIT returns the
computer to text mode.

{ ATARI PILOT has an imaginary pen that draws on the screen. This pen has three
colors: red, yellow, and blue. GRAPHICS automatically uses yellow unless you
change the pen color with the PEN command. The following is an example chang-

j ing the pen color to red.

Example:

| GR: PEN RED
The PEN can also erase colors and lines; simply choose the ERASE color and redraw
over the area you want erased.

i GR: PEN ERASE
If you want to move the pen across the screen without drawing any lines, specify UP:

q & GR: PEN UP
This is different from ERASE because it doesn’t erase anything.
