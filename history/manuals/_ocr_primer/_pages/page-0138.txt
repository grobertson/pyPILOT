I There is one more type of PEN subcommand: the PEN UP subcommand. PEN UP
tells the computer to lift the imaginary pen off the screen. This is different than PEN
ERASE because PEN ERASE erases over other graphics, whereas PEN UP moves
| over the graphics screen without erasing the display.
@ The PEN subcommand does not draw anything. It simply selects a color or raises
or lowers the imaginary pen. Therefore, when you issue a PEN subcommand, you
| won't see any change in the screen display until you make the pen draw with one
of the following subcommands. @
After using the PEN UP subcommand, you must assign a PEN color with the PEN
i subcommand before the PEN will show on the graphics screen again.
