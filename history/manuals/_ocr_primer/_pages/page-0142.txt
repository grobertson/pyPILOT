SUBCOMMANDS USING TURTLE GEOMETRY
4 The TURNTO Subcommand
The TURNTO subcommand sets the angle THETA to a value in degrees. This, in effect,
| points the turtle in the specified THETA angle.
The format for the TURNTO subcommand is:
I GR: TURNTO degree angle
Example:
GR: TURNTO 90
4 GR: TURNTO 180
