REPEATING GRAPHICS COMMANDS
q You can repeat one or more GRAPHICS subcommands by enclosing them within
parentheses, (). A numeric expression placed in front of the left parenthesis tells
the computer how many times to repeat the GRAPHICS subcommands.
5 The format for repeating GRAPHICS subcommands is:
GR: numeric expression (subcommands)
where: numeric expression represents the number of times to repeat the sub-
commands
subcommands are the subcommands to be repeated. If there are
i more than one, they must be separated by semicolons
Examples:
GR: 180(DRAW 1; TURN 2)
j GR: 10(DRAW 20; TURN — 36)
GR: 360(DRAW 1; TURN 1)
