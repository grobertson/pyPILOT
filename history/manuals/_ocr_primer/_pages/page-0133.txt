THE GRAPHICS The GRAPHICS command tells the computer to enter graphics mode and open the |
COMMAN D, GR: _ 8raphics screen.
The format for the GRAPHICS command is:
GR: subcommand
Because there are several different types of graphics capabilities, they can’t all be |
handled under one command without the help of subcommands. The graphic sub-
commands tell the computer what to do in graphics mode.
