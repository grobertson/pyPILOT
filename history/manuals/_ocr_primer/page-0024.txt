i € CORRECTING MISTAKES
There are three methods to correct spelling or typing mistakes when talking to your
computer on the keyboard. They are:
1. Retype the entire line.
2. Backspace to the mistake and retype the rest of the line.
3. Use cursor control keys to correct the mistake. This method is for advanced
programmers and is discussed in Appendix C.
