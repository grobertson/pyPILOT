4 SENSING THE PADDLE TRIGGER
The paddle triggers, like the joystick triggers, have two values consisting of:
O if trigger is up
| 1 if trigger is depressed
To create a Paddle Trigger Sensing Program, use the Paddle Sensing Program and
replace lines 10 and 30 with the following:
| e 10 R: PADDLE TRIGGER SENSING
30T: PADDLE #1 TRIGGER = %TO
