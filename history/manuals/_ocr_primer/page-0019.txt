The keyboard is similar to a typewriter. To type the character on the top half of the
key, you must press and hold the key while you press the key. To type the
character on the bottom of the key, simply press the key.
The keyboard is made up of five types of characters: alphabetic, numeric, punctua-
tion, graphics, and cursor control. The alphabetic characters (letters A through Z},
numbers (0 through 9), and punctuation characters are clearly marked on the key
caps. To type lowercase (small) letters, press the key first to put the
computer in lowercase mode. To change to uppercase letters, press the
key and the key.
