THE PAUSE COMMAND, PA:
The PAUSE command tells the computer to pause, or delay program execution tem- |
porarily. PAUSE does not affect the sound in progress; the sound continues playing
while the computer pauses before executing the next statement. The length of the
pause determines the length of the tone. |
The format for the PAUSE command is:
PA: numeric expression a |
Examples:
PA: 2 |
PA: #K *5/2
PA: 32
