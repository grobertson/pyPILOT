Lines 40 and 50 recompute the values to two different random numbers. At line, 60
| SO: #A, #B, the first voice plays #A. The second voice plays #B. The PAUSE com-
mand at line 70 generates a random length of a pause between0 and 31,varying the

| loop again. To stop this program, press the key.
