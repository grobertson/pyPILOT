Once listed, you can check the program for mistakes, and then change, add, or
delete lines from the program. The various ways to erase, replace, or insert lines
from within a program will be discussed at the end of this section.
The LIST command lists entire programs or portions of a program, depending upon
how you enter the LIST command:
LIST 10,50 displays the entire program
LIST 10 displays line 10
LIST 30 displays line 30 |
LIST 20,40 displays lines 20 through 40
LIST 10,20 displays lines 10 through 20
@ When entering two line numbers, the first line number must be smaller than the
second line number. If you list a line number not actually in the program, your
ATARI computer responds with a blank line. &@
