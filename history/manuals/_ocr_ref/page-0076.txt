GR:TURNTO

Turn to N Degrees e
The command GR:TURNTO N tells the turtle to turn in place until he is heading toward
the angle N degrees. The top of the screen is 0 degree. Positive angles (+) are measured
clockwise from 0 degree; negative angles (-) are measured counterclockwise.
Since the turtle is invisible on the screen, you cannot see him turn, but you can see the
direction of his new heading by executing a GO, DRAW, or FILL command after the
TURNTO. Figure 4-15 shows a program that uses TURNTO to let you make a design on
the screen with right angles and 10-unit lines.
